### Project Overview
Developed a split learning framework for diabetes risk prediction across heterogeneous clinical tabular datasets.

### Key Highlights
* Designed and evaluated logit-level and encoder-level distillation strategies under extreme few-shot supervision.
* Benchmarked MLP, SAINT, and TabM student models, achieving consistent gains over scratch training in low-resource settings.
* Addressed data heterogeneity and client privacy in distributed clinical environments.
