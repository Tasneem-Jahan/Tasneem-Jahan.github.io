I am a Master's graduate in Computer Science and Engineering from BRAC University, specializing in machine learning, distributed learning systems, and healthcare informatics. My research experience focuses on machine learning, deep learning, knowledge distillation, and learning from limited and heterogeneous data. My MSc thesis explored split learning frameworks for heterogeneous clinical tabular data, where I evaluated logit-level and encoder-level knowledge distillation under few-shot supervision using MLP, SAINT, and TabM models. My BSc thesis investigated machine learning approaches for heart disease prediction using multi-source clinical datasets, applying cross-validation, hyperparameter tuning, and ROC-AUC-based model evaluation. 

In addition to research, I have a strong foundation in full-stack software development and data engineering, with experience in Python, relational databases, and modern web frameworks.

Research Interests
======
My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning and healthcare, with a particular focus on agentic AI systems for clinical decision support. I aim to develop multi-agent retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) frameworks that integrate multimodal clinical data, including electronic health records, medical imaging, and clinical notes, while remaining robust and generalizable across diverse patient populations. A central theme of my work is uncertainty quantification and model calibration, ensuring that clinical agents reliably flag their limitations rather than producing overconfident predictions in high-stakes environments. I am also driven by questions of algorithmic fairness and bias mitigation, specifically investigating how models trained on observational healthcare data may exploit non-clinical proxy variables (such as insurance status or socioeconomic markers) in ways that exacerbate existing disparities in care. Ultimately, my goal is to bridge the gap between foundational deep learning research and deployable, clinician-trusted tools that enhance diagnostic accuracy and improve patient outcomes in real-world settings.

Featured Projects & Research
======
* **Knowledge Distillation in Split Learning for Heterogeneous Clinical Tabular Data:** Proposed a split learning framework for diabetes risk prediction across heterogeneous clinical tabular datasets. Designed and evaluated logit-level and encoder-level distillation strategies under extreme few-shot supervision, benchmarking MLP, SAINT, and TabM student models to achieve consistent gains over scratch training. [[Thesis]](https://dspace.bracu.ac.bd/items/fcb0c1f9-183f-4bd4-aba8-3f915c5e4c58)
* **Heart Disease Prediction Using Machine Learning:** Conducted a comparative study of five machine learning classifiers (KNN, Naïve Bayes, SVM, Logistic Regression, and Random Forest) on integrated multi-source clinical datasets using 10-fold cross-validation, hyperparameter tuning, and ROC-AUC analysis.
* **E-Commerce Customer Retention & Behavioral Segmentation Engine:** Built an end-to-end analytics pipeline using multi-table SQL joins and aggregations on transactional data. Contrasted rule-based RFM quantile segmentation with machine learning-driven $K$-Means clustering, optimizing clusters via log-transformations, the Elbow Method, and a custom multi-metric Fitness Score.
* **Weather API Data Pipeline & Power BI Dashboard:** Engineered an automated Python ETL pipeline to ingest and transform hourly weather observations from the Open-Meteo API, performed SQL-based trend analysis on meteorological variables, and built an interactive Power BI dashboard for KPI tracking.
