I am a Master's graduate in Computer Science and Engineering from BRAC University, specializing in machine learning, distributed learning systems, and healthcare informatics. My research centers on developing privacy-preserving machine learning frameworks, knowledge distillation, and split learning paradigms for complex and heterogeneous clinical tabular datasets.

In addition to research, I have a strong foundation in full-stack software development and data engineering, with experience in Python, relational databases, and modern web frameworks.

Research Interests
======
* **Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning:** Split learning, federated setups, and privacy leakage mitigation in distributed clinical domains.
* **Knowledge Distillation:** Logit-level and representation-level transfer learning under extreme few-shot and low-resource constraints.
* **Clinical Informatics & Tabular AI:** Deep tabular architectures (MLP, SAINT, TabM) applied to clinical risk prediction and cohort modeling.
* **Data Engineering & Analytics:** End-to-end ETL pipelines, data segmentation, and statistical modeling.
