**Institution:** American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB)  
**Project Type:** B.Sc. Undergraduate Thesis  

### Project Overview
Conducted an empirical comparative study evaluating machine learning algorithms to accurately predict heart disease risk across multi-source clinical healthcare datasets.

### Key Highlights
* **Model Benchmarking:** Evaluated and compared five supervised classifiers: $K$-Nearest Neighbors (KNN), Naïve Bayes, Support Vector Machines (SVM), Logistic Regression, and Random Forest.
* **Cross-Validation & Optimization:** Applied 10-fold cross-validation alongside systematic hyperparameter tuning to ensure generalization and prevent overfitting on clinical data.
* **Evaluation Metrics:** Selected the optimal predictive model based on comprehensive ROC-AUC analysis and classification metrics across competing algorithms.
